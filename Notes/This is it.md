## §5 The arithmetic (so no number is ever argued from memory)
- **A $500 fixed job → ≈$310 in ACB (62%)** base case: 10% fee + 10% VAT on it, Upwork's VN withholding (5% VAT + 2% PIT on gross), $0.99 DLB + ~1.5% FX, Connects amortised at 40 proposals × 14 per win. **$283** at a 15% fee · **$210–260** if the tax office taxes it as wages · **$192** at 80 proposals per hire. An ongoing **$30/h contract nets ≈$23/h.**
- **$3,000 net by Apr 8** = 129–229 billable hours (at $30–20/h) or ~10 × $500 wins. Committed motion = 78–130 proposals → 2–3 hires at the 40-per-hire base → a few hundred dollars at posted medians. ==The yes-case is one client buying 8–11 h/wk at $30/h from January; Tam's longest visible contracts ran 108–163 days, so it exists, and it is not the base case.==
- **4 h/wk on Upwork = $262–682/mo net.** $3k/mo at 4 h/wk = **$214/h gross**; 2.3% of frontend posts pay ≥$75/h, none ≥$150/h. ==This is why the end state is an asset, and why the asset is a subscription.==
- **The floor in subscribers:** 30–40M VND ≈ $1,150–1,540 → 7–9 subs at $171/mo before Paddle (~5% + fees) and tax.
- **Tax line everyone forgets:** $3k/mo net ≈ $3.7k gross ≈ **1.15B VND/yr > the 1B VND exemption** (Decree 141/2026) → the $3k life is also a business-registration + e-invoicing decision. Upwork already withholds 5% VAT + 2% PIT from Vietnam-resident individuals plus 10% VAT on its fees. Regime A (registered business individual) ≈8% of gross; Regime B (unregistered, taxed as wages on top of salary, Jul 2026 guidance under Decree 253/2026) ≈21–27%. ==Planning number (ruled 2026-10-09): 21–27% until irrefutable proof, then register as a business individual (~8%) for the long run. A tax adviser before the first payout is on the board either way.==
- **The planning number applied:** every net figure above is Regime A. At 21–27% read a $500 job as **$210–260**, a $30/h contract as **≈$18–19/h**, 4 h/wk on Upwork as **≈$210–550/mo**, the quit floor as **≈10–13 subscribers** and $3k/mo as **≈25**. Plan on these; treat anything better as the registration's upside.
- **What the salary is:** the cheapest funding the evidence supports (Antifragile barbell: safe leg = 43M gross at Edge8; convex leg = this route). The post-quit line, if Oct 10 2027 passes: Upwork stays at ≤2 proposals/wk as the insurance leg for 6 months; the audience channel (text) keeps compounding; the next asset is picked from the same demand log, never from a hunt.

## §9 Lessons that are law here (from [[Books/Million Dollar Weekend]], [[Tips & Tools/Entrepreneurship]], [[Books/Skin In The Game]], [[Books/Antifragile]])
- ==Don't spend another dime until you make your first dollar== → $300 is Connects, nothing else; the demo repo costs $0; Paddle costs $0 to open.
- ==No ask, no get== → the scoreboard counts proposals, not commits. 293 commits and 0 asks is the pattern this file exists to break.
- ==Keep the day job until there is tangible evidence; the safe trigger is recurring revenue ≈ monthly expenses, growing== → Oct 10 2027's rule, verbatim from your own note.
- ==The entry must be a strenuous process, not an event== → no launch day; a route with six dated gates.
- ==The marketplace, not you, decides viability== → the demand log picks the product; "I'll build it and see" is the named anti-pattern.
- ==You want maximal free time, not maximal activity== → the owned test measures hours, not features. ==Survival first== → the renewal is the default, the quit is earned.
- ==What matters is how much you make when you are right, not how often== → one client at 8–11 h/wk or one product that repeats pays for all the silent proposals.
- Barbell: the salary is the safe leg; the convex leg is capped at 20 h and $300 so it cannot blow you up, only delay you.
