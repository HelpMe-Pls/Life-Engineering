## Recommendation for THIS sprint
**USD = Paddle.** Fully verified chain (eligibility, approval windows, payout calendar, payout methods, fees), $0 to open, three payout methods, and the portfolio runbooks already assume it. Its two weaknesses are scheduling facts, not eligibility: payouts batch monthly (created on the 1st, sent by the 15th, ≥$100), so **sales must close by Nov 30 to be cash by Dec 15** — December sales pay in January; and the domain review needs a live product page. **Switch trigger:** if Paddle verification is not complete 7 business days after submission, apply to Dodo Payments (1–3 business days, individual flow documented) — it costs $0 and nothing is lost. Not re-checked this pass: Paddle's accepted-product rules (software/digital vs services or courses) — confirm against Paddle's terms if the offer is a course or a service; Stripe Managed Payments, for comparison, excludes "Professional services, such as consulting" and "live 1-1 coaching".
**VND = PayOS → ACB (already live).** Nothing to open; re-prove it.
**Budget screen under the updated R9 (≤$20, domain only):** Paddle, Dodo Payments, Polar, Creem, PayPal Vietnam and PayOS cost $0 to open — in budget. Stripe via Atlas ($500 + $100/yr) is over budget and stays in the table for completeness only. Payoneer is excluded as a payout leg because its $29.95 low-volume annual fee alone exceeds R9. Polar's $2/month active-payout pass-through is a recurring fixed cost and is flagged. The ACB USD account is in budget only if the branch confirms no minimum-deposit or maintenance fee; until that is confirmed, Paddle's payout method is PayPal.
