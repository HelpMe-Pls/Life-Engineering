## Demand-supply gap per marketplace (one line each)

- **Shopify — invoicing.** Widest verified gap in this brief. Both category leaders sit at 4.9★, yet their 2026 one-star reviews cluster on the same three complaints: plan auto-upgraded by order count ("not able to manually select your plan", Will & Bear, 2026-06-16), charged after uninstall / when unused (Perfect Airsoft 2026-04-04; Plumtree Baby 2026-04-21), credit notes paywalled ("$7 starter plan not possible… credit notes only in professional plan", GeurFris.nl, 2026-07-02). Nobody sells a flat-price invoice + credit-note setup. Merchants are named with country and date on the review pages.
- **Obsidian — AI plugins are saturated** ($14.99–$30/mo, four vendors above). The only demand signal for something *else* is the forum thread asking for a paid market to fix unmaintained plugins ([forum](https://forum.obsidian.md/t/paid-plugin-market-and-how-to-solve-unmaintained-plugins/109137)) — a signal, not a measured gap; Obsidian's CEO has said no official paid marketplace will be built (same thread cluster). UNVERIFIED as a gap.
- **VS Code, Chrome, Gumroad:** category pages did not render → gap UNVERIFIED. **Raycast, Setapp:** no paid supply to measure. **Claude/MCP:** no in-listing prices exist, so no in-listing gap can be measured.

## What this means against R3 (Oct 18)

1. No marketplace *listing* produces a stranger dollar by itself inside 31 days; the ones that list fast (Obsidian 24h, VS Code same day) have zero built-in demand capture, and the one with built-in demand (Shopify) cannot be listed in 14 days.
2. The money-in-31-days move that survives C4 is: **use a marketplace's public review pages as the named list**, sell the fix outside the marketplace (own checkout via Paddle/Dodo — Shopify custom apps cannot bill through Shopify anyway), and treat the Obsidian / VS Code listing as an install path only if the product needs one.
3. Rails: nothing here changes RAILS-INTEL. New fact: Lemon Squeezy's own docs list Vietnam for bank payouts today — usable as a stop-gap checkout, still not a plan.
