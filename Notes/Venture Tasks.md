> - **Why it's still peak-intent (the real mechanism, not hype):** a duty rate is **changing on a hard, calendar-fixed, statutory date** every affected importer knows about, **plus** an unresolved question of what replaces it → the whole segment recomputes landed cost *that week*. A dated legal cliff, not a news cycle.
