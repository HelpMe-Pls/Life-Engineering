# 🚨 THE RUNBOOK — the only part that exists during the meeting

**Legend:** *italics = say it exactly* · **(…)** = shut up, count 4 · 🦴 = spine move, fire the lowest unfired

**4 lines to know cold (say them exactly like this):**
- 🥇 **The band:** *"I've done my research and found that the market rate for my role right now is 46.7 to 59.5, realistically speaking"*
- 🥈 **The gate:** ==*"How am I supposed to sign another year at far below market rate when I'm capable of doing THREE people's work?"*==
- 🗝️ **The severance** (whenever staying gets used against you): *"I do want to stay. And what I sign is a contract, and contracts get priced properly."*
- 🥉 **The walk:** *"I'm sorry. I'm afraid I just can't do that."* (then NOTHING. Not one more word.)

### 🦴 THE SPINE — the meeting in order. He can move the fight; he cannot move the spine.

==You DRIVE seven bones: 1 · 3 · 5 · 7 · 8 · 14 · 15 — plus the bridge between 7 and 8.== Every other 🦴 is a counter: ==no cue, no fire, no guilt== — it waits, and skipping it costs nothing. "Lowest unfired 🦴" = the lowest DRIVE bone still standing.
