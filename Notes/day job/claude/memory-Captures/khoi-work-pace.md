Khôi can realistically spend at most 4 hours a day on tickets, ideally 2, as a deliberate guard against burnout (said 2026-09-23). A five-day sprint is therefore ~1.5–2.5 HT-days total. The ticket count cap was 5 per sprint until 2026-10-07, when he raised it: "for the tickets, we can raise their cap to maximum of 10." The cap counts every ticket in the sprint (V1.1, Canada, Events, anything), not just Events tickets; he corrected this the same day when it was filed as an Events rule. The hours cap did not change. Dave's rule makes every card one 4-hour block ([[edge8-sprint-card-rules]]), so cards can no longer be shrunk to fit more of them. Size and sequence work to that, but do not mention the constraint in ticket text.
