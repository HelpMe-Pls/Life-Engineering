Every spoken line in a demo script (the review pages' .line text) is written for the ear: short sentences of one idea each, everyday words and phrases, contractions, and the word the screen shows when there is one. No colon, semicolon or bracket inside a spoken line, and no turn of phrase that only works in writing. Khôi's own accent spellings (dah, dis, den, dat) stay.

Example he approved on 26 Oct 2026, the 3:55 line: "For a new chapter, we do a dry run first. It shows us what it would copy, but it doesn't actually copy anything." He rejected "it first only looks: it counts what it would copy, and copies nothing" and then "it just looks at first" as unnatural. "Dry run" also matches the GitHub box on screen.

**Why:** he reads the lines aloud live to EO; a written-style line trips him up and sounds scripted.

**How to apply:** draft or change any spoken line this way and read it aloud in your head before keeping it. The rule is also in the `# Sprint review prep` prompt (the script paragraph and the first-time viewer lens) in the vault's Notes/Daily Standup.md. See [[sprint-review-files]].
