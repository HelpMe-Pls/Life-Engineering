**Scoping rule, proved on 16 Oct 2026 (Sprint 41 Card 12).** With Deals and Contacts View set to "Their team's" and the Unassigned box off, a user sees a record when the record owner's Main Team is the user's Main Team, or when the record's "Shared teams" field names the user's team (property `hs_shared_team_ids`, "Additional teams whose users can access the record based on their permissions. This can be set manually or through Workflows or APIs."; sibling `hs_shared_user_ids`). Reports and dashboards obey the same rule as the deal and contact lists, so one dashboard with no chapter filter shows each chapter manager only their chapter's records. Unowned records with no shared team are invisible to them; the Unassigned tick would show every unowned record in the portal instead.

- **Main Team vs Extra Teams.** The Teams tab's "Team members" picker adds users as Extra members only. Settings > Users & Teams > click the user > Setup tab > Teams sets Main Team and Extra Teams. HubSpot's UI text: extra team members "can see the team's assigned content and assets. They're left out of any team reports." Observed: as an Extra member of EO Adelaide with no Main Team, the deals list showed Adelaide's 665 deals while the dashboards still showed Vietnam's numbers. That observation fell inside the dashboard lag below, so it does not prove the UI text; the 17 Oct demo page makes no claim about Extra Teams.

**Why:** rolling one Monday view out to every chapter, and the fix for the hidden unowned deals, both hinge on these mechanics, which cost an evening of logins to establish.

**How to apply:** to make a chapter's unowned deals and contacts visible to its manager, share them with the chapter team (Shared teams, by a workflow from Assigned Chapter and Prospective Chapter) or give them an owner on that team. Set a chapter manager's Main Team, never only Extra Teams. After any team change, wait and start a new login before judging a dashboard. See [[apac-regional-dashboard]], [[sow1-priority2-scoping]].

First real proof = memberships from the next nightly sync in the workflow history (passed 30 Oct: Sunny Yin, EO Adelaide). **Scalable layer, 30 Oct:** each Chapter record's own Shared teams holds its team, and the Altai sync's chapter-teams slice (PR #257, merged) appends it nightly to every current primary member's contact (re-read before write, never removes; `chapter-teams.fixed.<chapter>` = the daily miss check). New chapter = set its Chapter record's Shared teams; no workflow edit needed. A HubSpot workflow token can't do this (tokens offer Replace only, no Append).

Open from the research: Relationship records (spouse and child names, every chapter) show in chapter staff search with no share, so the Chapter staff set likely lets them see all of them; spouse contacts carry no chapter and stay hidden; is_member is never set back to false, so ex-members stay shared.

**Parent team (read 24 Oct 2026, Regional dashboard as Arlene on Main Team APAC Region):** a parent team sees the deals its child chapter teams OWN, and not deals that are only shared with a child team (Queensland and Sydney no-owner deals missing). So a region shows a shared-only deal only once it has an owner on the chapter team.
