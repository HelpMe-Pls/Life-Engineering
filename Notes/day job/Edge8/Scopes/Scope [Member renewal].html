EO HubSpot — Member Renewal plan

Built for: the Membership Chair and the Finance Chair

By: Khôi, Edge8 · written 9 Oct 2026 · updated 17 Oct 2026

Edge8’s draft statement of work asks for a renewal board of HubSpot deals, records that move through stages.

Status: built, all off. On 17 Oct 2026 Khôi built the board, three fields, five workflows and the report. They send members nothing. Only super admins (full-access HubSpot users) can use the board until EO’s membership team releases it.

User stories: none of EO’s 9 picked renewal stories is covered in full; the first changes are below, the rest in the gap list.

Renewal deals would change 19,948 members’ lifecycle stage to Opportunity

Two HubSpot settings, on for every board, set lifecycle stage, which EO’s reports count: a new deal sets Opportunity, a won deal Customer. By HubSpot’s count on 17 Oct, that moves 19,948 members to Opportunity, then up to 19,982 to Customer. Four of EO’s deal workflows, now off, need the filter “Pipeline is none of Member Renewal” before they go on, and a fifth must stay off. Renewals stay off until the fix is in place.

To stop this: by mid-March 2027 Khôi turns on the Lifecycle workflow, which sets Opportunity on new member deals only, then EO switches the created setting off, in that order. The won setting is EO’s choice before renewals start.

Renewals run from 1 April

  1. 1 April: deals open, and the Membership chair marks likely renewals.
  2. 1 May: deals go to Renewed or Dues due.
  3. 1 July: the rest go to Grace, then Renewed or Churned a year later.

What we build: the board, 4 fields, 5 workflows and the report, all built

  • The renewal board, built: the Member Renewal pipeline, with Check-in, Dues due, Grace, then Renewed or Churned.
  • Four fields, built: on each deal, Renewal due date, which carries the fiscal year, Likely to renew and Primary chapter. On each member, Latest membership end date.
  • Five workflows, built: Open, Dues due, Close, Grace end and Lifecycle.
  • No survey or email: EO’s membership team sends its own. The 3 survey questions wait for EO’s call: how the year went, whether they will renew, and what would make next year worth it.
  • One report, built: “Chapter: Renewal dues by stage”, the finance chair’s Monday view, private until release.
