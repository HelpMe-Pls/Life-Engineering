Watch out: the warm boss gets the quick yes

  • The warm boss. Kindness makes pushing feel like ingratitude. Give no yes to a start date in the room unless the record is part of it.
  • A promise is not the record. A date he says moves your start date. It does not replace what VssID shows.
  • Never split it. Half the months, half your time, both projects at once. Each one is the set-up that ended in the letter.
  • Words you never say. Owe, debt, withholding, illegal, lawyer, condition, non-negotiable, or else, why, sorry, the letter. Other people's insurance, the people who left, your lost sleep. Say the record, the gap, the catch-up.

The law protects the record and the contract, and no text was found that backs refusing work in your job · never said aloud

This is Claude's reading of the law's text, not legal advice. Three Claude agents read the texts on Mon 23 Nov 2026, and a fourth checked each claim against the text. Each row says how far that check went. Never say any of it aloud, because it turns a talk about a set-up into a threat. Before you stop work or file anything, ask a labour lawyer who has read your contract.

The point What the text says Where it is
Refusing work in your job because the insurance is unpaid No text was found that allows it. Unpaid insurance is not named in the list of cases where an employee may leave without notice. Pay that is not paid in full or on time is a listed case, and the text does not settle whether your deducted but unpaid share counts as that. An employee who ends a contract outside the listed cases ends it unlawfully. Confirmed for the list. That no other text allows a refusal is a search result, not proof. Labour Code 45/2019, Arts. 35, 39 and 40
A move to another project, with the same title and workplace The law gives no direct answer. The employer has the right to arrange and manage work. The next two rows apply only if the new work differs from the job in your contract, or changes one of its terms. Not confirmed. This is one agent's reading, and the checker did not read Art. 6. Labour Code, Arts. 6, 29 and 33
Work that differs from the contract The employer may move you to it for at most 60 working days in 1 year. It needs a sudden difficulty, or a business need that the company's labour rules list. It takes 3 working days' notice, with the length stated. Past 60 days it needs your written consent. The new pay is at least 85% of the old pay, and the old pay holds for the first 30 working days. Confirmed. Labour Code, Arts. 29 and 118
A change from remote to on site The workplace follows the contract unless both sides agree otherwise. A term of the contract changes only by agreement, in an appendix or a new contract, with 3 working days' notice. With no agreement the signed contract continues. An employee who is not given the agreed workplace may leave without notice. Confirmed for the change rule and the leave rule. The workplace sentence comes from one agent's read of Art. 28 and is not confirmed. Labour Code, Arts. 28, 33 and 35
The warning letter The law has four discipline forms: a reprimand, a pay-rise delay of up to 6 months, demotion and dismissal. It names no form called a warning letter. If yours was in fact a reprimand, the procedure in this row applies to it. If not, it is not a discipline form. Which one it is has not been confirmed. Discipline needs proof of fault from the employer, your presence with the right to defend yourself, and written minutes. It must come within 6 months of the act. Confirmed for the procedure and the 6 months. The four forms matched what the checker read. Labour Code, Art. 122, and Arts. 123 and 124
Ending your contract Refusing an assignment is not in the list of grounds for dismissal. But the employer may end a contract when the employee often fails to complete the work, judged by criteria in its own written rules. On a term of 12 to 36 months that takes 30 days' notice. If the ending is unlawful, the employer must take you back, pay the wages and insurance for the days you did not work, and add at least 2 months of wages. Confirmed. Labour Code, Arts. 36, 41 and 125
Overtime Overtime needs your consent. It pays at least 150% on a normal day, 200% on a weekly rest day and 300% on a public holiday. Confirmed. Labour Code, Arts. 98 and 107
The insurance money The employer must deduct your share, add its own and pay both by the last day of the next month. When it pays late, it owes the full amount plus 0.03% a day. It must compensate you for harm caused by unpaid or incomplete insurance. Late payment becomes evasion 60 days after the deadline, once the agency has sent a written reminder. Confirmed. Two fines are not confirmed. Press reports say the 2026 penalty decree adds 12% to 15% of the late amount for late payment, and the checker could not read that article itself. The fine for evasion, 18% to 20%, matched in one check, and another check could not read its article. Law on Social Insurance 41/2024, Arts. 13, 34, 38 and 39. Decree 274/2025. Decree 283/2026, Art. 43
Unemployment insurance if a contract ends The benefit needs 12 paid months in the 24 months before the contract ends. The employer must pay the unpaid months in full when the contract ends. If it did not, it must pay you a sum equal to the benefit you would have got. The benefit is 60% of your average insured salary, for 3 months after 12 to 36 paid months. Confirmed. Employment Law 74/2025, Arts. 33, 38 and 39
Your rights and the steps You may ask the social insurance agency to confirm your contribution record, require the employer to carry out its duties in full, and complain, denounce and sue. The steps: ask the employer in writing, then report to the agency so that it sends its written reminder, then go to the labour arbitration council or the court. The agency sends reminders in the first 10 days of a month. An insurance dispute needs no conciliation step first. Confirmed after two corrections. Law 41/2024, Arts. 10 and 35. Decree 274/2025, Art. 3. Labour Code, Art. 188
The time limit A labour dispute goes to the court within 1 year of the day you learn of the breach, and to the labour arbitration council within 9 months. Confirmed. Whether each unpaid month restarts that period was not researched. The contract is 13 months old, so for the first unpaid months both periods may already be over or close to it. Ask a lawyer this week. Labour Code, Art. 190
When it becomes a crime Evasion by deceit or another trick for 6 months or more, after the employer was already fined for it and still does not pay, with 50 million to under 300 million VND unpaid, or 10 to under 50 employees affected. Larger sums and more employees fall in the heavier clauses, and so does keeping contributions already deducted from employees. Confirmed after a correction. A 2025 amendment to the Penal Code was not checked. Penal Code, Art. 216

Not confirmed, so not in the table: which body takes a denunciation and its phone number, the protection from retaliation for a person who denounces, and the health insurance rate.
