## 0. Why this shape (read once, then run Session 0)
- **Your own law demands it.** [[Contingencies]] § levers: a new hunt needs a re-grill of the constraint set first — "the constraints, not the market, were the binding wall in 2026". Session 0 *is* that re-grill. The sprint is the pro-cyclical next-option engine ([[EP SaaS]] §11), **not** Venture 4; the OPEN-SLOT rulings stay untouched, so nothing here relitigates July.
- **July was idea-first + faceless pull.** 17/17 candidates died on clone-floods and free incumbents; FB groups and subreddits ban you or bury you (your evidence, 2026-09-17). So this sprint is **channel-first, push**: start from lists of buyers you can legally message under your own name, find what they already pay for, sell that. A product is chosen for the buyers you can reach, never the other way round.
- **Your own dossier already ruled on 60-day windows.** FACE-MODE-DOSSIER (2026-07-18): a cold product venture inside a 60-day break-glass window is "triple-graveyarded"; the Hail Mary "sells the assets (services, consulting, the operated Clock lane), it does not launch products." KILL-TABLE meta-finding 6: "The wall is the faceless/no-audience doctrine × the $3k NET ceiling × 2026's clone-flooded pull channels." The sprint moves exactly those three levers (R4, R2, R5) and lets a service or an existing asset win the scorecard if it scores higher. "Preferably new" is a tie-breaker, not a filter.
- **The failure mode is reps, not research.** Three Sunday rows, then six weeks without one; zero asks to strangers across three ventures; ~1.1M research tokens and a 97KB dossier, nobody contacted. Hence: every prompt ends in a `NEXT ASK`, research runs AFK and time-boxed, the council **ranks but cannot veto**, and nothing gets built before somebody pays except the page that takes the money.
- **Realism, stated once.** $1k MRR in October from a cold start is not a plan. First stranger dollar by Oct 18 and $3,000 cash by Dec 31 *is* — if ≥30 asks/week leave your hands from Sep 28. Existing assets (Clock's finished research, the EP platform with you as the instructor, your own skills) enter the candidate list on equal footing with new ideas; "new" is a preference, money is the target, the scorecard decides. Your own base rate (Antifragile §11): median 12–18 months to $1K MRR, and every named comp ran full-time — which is why this plan sells before it builds.
