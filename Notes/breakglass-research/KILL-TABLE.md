## What the 17 kills say as one dataset (the meta-findings)

1. **Free-incumbent / first-party killed ~13 of 17.** In every wedge thin enough for a solo, either a free tool already does the job or the platform/state ships it natively (Atlassian, Figma generative plugins, USCIS alerts, UK PRS database, Stripe Tax, Sentry Crons, HomeBinder).
2. **The AI-slop storm eats the entrant before it feeds him.** Clone-floods verified in EVERY discoverable niche ≤12 months old (EAA scanners, cron monitors, tender alerts, VAT APIs, exam-prep farms, case trackers, home logs; 700 AI plugins/week at wp.org alone). ==The disorder Venture 4 was designed to feed on is precisely what closes the faceless-pull channels it would need.== Discovery still works; monetization against $0 is what dies.
3. **Filter 7 is near-unsatisfiable in 2026** — verifiable FACELESS-indie revenue ≤12–24mo barely exists as a public dataset: successes that publish numbers are audience-driven by construction (build-in-public), and quiet successes don't publish. Named as an epistemic limit, not an excuse: the bar stood, and 17 candidates failed it.
4. **Rails are NOT the binding constraint** — see RAILS-INTEL (verified VN-workable: Apple, Google Play, Atlassian, Figma, Freemius, Dodo, Paddle; verified broken/failing: Gumroad-probable, Lemon Squeezy→Stripe, RapidAPI).
5. **Counter-cyclical demand exists and was found** (budgeting, trade-exam enrollment 2008–10, tender volumes, USCIS anxiety) — ==it is captured by incumbents with free tiers or accumulated-data moats, not by new faceless entrants.==
6. **The one live counterexample defines the binding constraint:** BoltAI — a solo developer in Ho Chi Minh City at a reported $30k/mo total (Mar 2025, Indie Hackers) — got there via going viral on X, THEN Setapp. Geography, solo-ness, and stack were never the wall. ==The wall is the faceless/no-audience doctrine × the $3k NET ceiling × 2026's clone-flooded pull channels.==
