## 0.5 What you sell, in one story
Trần's Cooling & Heating / Mike's Electrical, 35 people. Law, insurance, and GC contracts require valid credentials: **state contractor/tech licenses (expire on state schedules), first-aid/CPR (2-year), OSHA site cards, insurance-mandated refreshers, and the new A2L-refrigerant training wave** — plus permanent records like EPA-608 that auditors still demand proof of. Sarah the office manager tracks it all in a spreadsheet; her nightmare is the silent lapse → inspector or GC audit finds it → fine, failed audit, tech pulled off site, lost contract. Renewals are worse than reminders: find training, chase the tech, collect the card, file the proof.
==Fact discipline: EPA-608 NEVER EXPIRES — it is a lifetime cert. Track it as a permanent audit record, never pitch it as a renewal. A real ops manager spots that fake in one glance and the demo dies.== The real 2026 tailwind to cite instead: the AIM Act leak-repair threshold dropped 50→15 lbs on Jan 1 2026 — more systems need certified techs, more audit exposure.

**What her company buys for ~$199/mo (one responsive web dashboard — deliberately NOT a mobile app):**
1. **The registry** — every employee × credential × expiry, red/amber/green. *(the hero)*
2. **The clock** — 90/60/30/7-day emails to worker AND supervisor. ==Nothing lapses silently again.== *(the hero, part 2)*
3. **The renewal, closed two ways** — *track lane*: reminder → external renewal → upload proof → registry updates (ALL cert types, day 1). *Train lane*: reminder link → lesson → quiz → cert auto-issues → ==registry flips green by itself== (the moat, where company-controlled training is allowed).

5. **The escrow** — weekly auto-export of the actual cert PDFs + a plain CSV index to *their* Google Drive. Never a manifest pointing at your servers. ==“If we vanish, you lose nothing.”==

Why they keep paying: the clock never stops; leaving = rebuilding the spreadsheet and losing the audit trail — plus the monthly named-save email (§6). ==You sell the absence of a $16,550 fine and 5 hours of her week — and you run it FOR her.==

## 5. The firewall — noise vs. signal (pre-decided; metrics on Sundays only)

| It happened | It is | You do |

| 10–30 asks, zero replies | Noise (1–5% reply is the median) | Keep asking; the unit of evidence is a fair cold run, not 20 sends |

| Gate reads 0 from a fair, unstarved cold run | **SIGNAL — the strongest** | Obey §4.6 (with its noise guard). No renegotiating |
| Tuesday-night despair | Noise (fatigue mimics insight) | No weekday decisions. Sunday, gates in hand |
