## 0.1 Why this is antifragile — the exact mechanics (one screen)
Fragile things lose from disorder; robust things ignore it; ==antifragile things gain from it.== This venture gains, seven ways:
1. **Demand rises with chaos.** Customers appear exactly when tariff policy convulses — and 2026 has produced a change a month. A calm year keeps a floor (the tariff schedule is revised every January; trade cases and audits never stop); a loud year multiplies signups at zero extra cost. ==Downside capped, upside open — that asymmetry is Taleb's definition.==
2. **The core asset is fed by disorder + time, not by your labor.** The collector records every federal tariff action daily, on cron. Each new shock adds rows only you hold with timestamps. Time — "the mother of all stressors" — makes this business stronger, not older.
3. **Barbell.** V1 + V2 are the income legs; this is the small wild bet. The total possible loss is written down in advance (~$30 domain + <$10/mo + ~60 hours + the $4K refund cap). The plausible payoff is orders of magnitude larger. Nothing in the middle.
4. **A stack of cheap options, never obligations.** Fewer than 5 subscribers in December? PARK — the archive keeps recording at ~$6/mo and you owe nobody anything. The app, the agency tier, the data API each get exercised only if reality invites them. ==You never have to predict whether the trade war continues — either answer leaves you fine; one leaves you rich.==
5. **Ruin amputated in advance (via negativa).** No customs filings (no license exposure), no personal data (no breach exposure), liability capped at fees, payment rail isolated from the siblings, no debt, no ads. Every fatal tail is cut before it can exist; what remains can only fail small and quiet.
6. **Errors make it stronger (hormesis).** A wrong estimate triggers the reconcile flow and a public correction in the changelog — each absorbed mistake raises trust, and every crisis in §11 already has its answer installed.
7. **It sells records, not predictions.** Forecasters get broken by surprises; a ledger cannot be wrong about the future because it only states the past, with the government's own text attached. ==This business never needs to be right about what happens next — only to write down what just happened, faster and more personally than anyone else.==

The honest limit: ==antifragile ≠ guaranteed.== It can still starve (nobody pays — G1 catches that in December, for pocket change). What it cannot do is blow up.
