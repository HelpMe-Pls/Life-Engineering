# CONTEXT — the words these files use (one line each)

> [!info] How to use this file
> The ubiquitous language of the Venture route. Every answer about the route uses these words with these meanings. Each line ends with where the word is defined: `R-nn` / `F-nn` / `U-nn` / `§n` point into [[This is it]] (the LAW); other sources are named. Add a line here the day a new word enters the LAW.

## Files
- **LAW** — the file [[This is it]]. It holds the verdict (§0), the rulings (§1), the route (§3), the firewall rows (§4), the calendar (§C) and the arithmetic (§5). Only Khoi edits it. (§A)
- **SCHEDULE** — the `# Venture` section of [[Weekly Tasks]]: the scoreboard, the demand log and its tally, the 8 dates, the notes. The skill writes it on Sunday. (§A)
- **MOVES** — [[Venture Tasks]]: the `## NOW` block and this week's boxes. (§A)
- **RECORD** — an older note kept for facts and history: [[EP SaaS]], [[Business Bank]], [[Contingencies]], [[Q4 Sprint]], the research folders. Never a plan source. (§A)
- **the skill** — `/long-vol-consultant`, the Sunday check-in wizard: eight pages, one keyboard dialog each (page 1 = the position and the keys, read-only), a draft held in the chat, one `y` at the end that writes the week back. `Esc` stops it with nothing written. Weeknights: `/long-vol-consultant log` (demand-log rows only) or `/long-vol-consultant <the blocker>` (the unblock pages only). (§10)
- **position.py** — the script beside the skill. It prints the phase, the week, the days to each gate, the last two rows and the open moves. It never writes. (§A, §C)

## The route
- **the route** — bid on Upwork, then productize: four phases from Fri Oct 9 2026 to Sun Oct 10 2027. (§3)
- **phase (P1–P4)** — P1 Stranger dollar · P2 Productize · P3 Shift weight · P4 Owned test. (§3)
- **gate** — a dated test with a pre-written rule for pass and for fail. Gates never move. The seven gate dates: Nov 15, Dec 7, Jan 6, Apr 8, May 20, Jul 10, Oct 10. (§4, §C)
- **firewall row (F-nn)** — one row of §4: a date or a trigger, and the rule that fires. "The only way a gate moves." (§4)
- **ruling (R-nn)** — one dated row of §1. Rulings move one at a time, consciously, never at 2am. (§1)
- **"never at 2am"** — no ruling is written late at night or in a mood. The skill writes none between 21:30 and 06:00. (§1, §A)
- **the frontier** — the list of open questions that still wait for an answer. "The frontier is empty" = no open question is left; every ruling is settled. (§1 closing line; "frontier question" in strength-research/README)
- **unknown (U-nn)** — a fact the research could not settle, with the action that settles it. It does not block the route. (§8)
- **the week** — Mon → Sun. Its Sunday row closes it. "Week N of a phase" = the Nth Sunday row of that phase. (§A, §C)
- **day N** — counted from the start day as day 1. Oct 9 = day 1, Dec 7 = day 60, Jan 6 = day 90. "Day 5" of a proposal = 4 days after you sent it. (§A, §6)
- **zero week** — a week with <3 proposals or no post. It "did not count"; nothing else changes. Week 1 (Oct 9–11) was one. (F-02, R-20)
- **target** — a deliverable date that may slip (the demo repo, Paddle live). It is not a gate. The live date is in MOVES. (§C)
- **the demo repo** — ONE public repo in the niche, ≤8 h: the conversion-sync worker with a README that reads as a landing page. The portfolio link in every proposal and the v0 of the product. Target Sun Nov 1 2026. (§3 P1)
- **the product (default)** — CRM → ad-platform conversion sync (HubSpot → Meta CAPI / Google Ads offline conversions), a monthly subscription on Paddle, $171/mo anchor. The demand log can replace it on Jan 6, once. (§3, R-14)
- **the asset** — the owned product and its subscription income. "Keep the asset running" = it stays live and billing at ≤4 h/wk after Oct 10 2027, even when you renew. (§3 P4, F-14)
- **owned** — income that still arrives ≥3 months after you stop selling and shipping. (R-05)
- **the owned test / the 13 weeks** — P4: MRR ≥ floor, and ≤4 h of logged upkeep in each of the 13 Sunday rows Jul 18 → Oct 10 2027. It starts Sat Jul 10 2027 only if F-12 fires. (§3 P4, §C)
- **the floor** — today's burn, 30–40M VND/mo (≈$1,150–1,540). The quit line, not the goal; $3k/mo is "the life wanted". (R-01)
- **MRR** — monthly recurring revenue after Paddle's fee, in USD. (scoreboard key)
- **renew / the renewal** — sign the next Edge8 employment contract on Sun Oct 10 2027 (43M gross, the safe leg). The planned default; the quit is a test outcome. (§0 Q13, §5, F-14)
- **quit-insurance** — a stranger's money in the bank by Jan 6 2027. Without it an Oct 2027 quit is off the table. (F-05)
- **the barbell** — the salary is the safe leg; the route is the convex leg, capped at 20 h/wk and $300 so it can only delay you, never blow you up. (§9)
- **Edge8** — the day job (43M VND gross). Its contract renews Sun Oct 10 2027. (§5, 1-1 Bench)
- **Tam HN** — the Vietnamese Upwork freelancer whose niche, not whose numbers, the route copies. Free masterclass Wed Oct 14 2026, 20:00. (§0, R-18)
- **the bidding window** — 20:00–22:00 HCMC = US-East morning; 21:00 after Nov 1. (§3 P1)

## The Sunday read
- **the Sunday read** — the 30-minute weekly meeting: type `/long-vol-consultant`. One scoreboard row, the governor, the signals, the one question, the unblock steps, the write-back. (SCHEDULE)
- **the scoreboard** — one row per Sunday in the SCHEDULE: `Sunday | hrs | Gov? | Upwork p/r/h/Σ$ | Asset s/MRR/u | Post | Signal`. Numbers and dashes only. (SCHEDULE)
- **hrs** — all venture hours that week: motion + delivery + asset. 20 is the ceiling. (R-02)
- **Gov? / the governor** — the Sunday yes/no: "did I break 20 h OR borrow sleep OR skip the row?" YES twice in a row = next week at HALF load. A skipped Sunday counts as YES. (F-01)
- **half load** — the coming week at ~10 h, cut in the cut order, pre-committed. (F-01)
- **the cut order** — when a week is tight, cut: 1. build hours on the demo / asset, 2. the text post, 3. NEVER the proposals, NEVER paid work. (SCHEDULE)
- **p/r/h/Σ$** — proposals sent / replies / hires this week / Σ net USD landed in ACB. (scoreboard key)
- **s/MRR/u** — Σ paying subscribers / MRR after Paddle's fee / upkeep hours this week. `-` until P2. (scoreboard key)
- **Post** — 1 if the weekly text post went out, else 0. (scoreboard key)
- **Signal** — the firewall row that fired this week, by ID, else `-`. A Signal is answered by its row, never improvised. (§10)
- **Σ · `-` · `0` · `_`** — running total · not live yet · live and nothing happened · fill on Sunday. (§A)
- **pull** — an unsolicited "can I pay?", a repeat client, or a referral. Pull beats plan: serve it first, inside the delivery cap. The EP instructor's return is the current pull. (F-15, R-20)
- **the delivery cap** — paid client hours per week: 0–10 h in P1, ≤8 h from Jan 7, ≤6 h from Apr 9. (§3, F-06)
- **motion** — 3–5 proposals in the bidding window + 1 text post. The first 3 h of the 20. Never cut. (R-09)
- **the one question** — "did money or evidence move this week?" The only consultant question. (§10)
- **the green-lumber trap** — a note that reads like analysis instead of reps: theorizing, re-planning, polishing. Named out loud; the cure is "close the file, send the next ask". (§10)
- **long-vol** — the posture: slack intact (20 h, sleep, the free evening), spike weeks refused, pull served, gates fixed. (the skill)
- **Fat-Tony-honest** — reps and money over theory and polish; an honest 0 is data. (the skill)
- **the invariants** — what no proposal may touch: 20 h/wk ceiling · $300 Connects-only · the venture-free evening · sleep beats streaks · the gate dates · the one-switch rules. (§10)
- **one-switch rules** — each switch happens once: the profile rewrite (F-04), the product switch (F-09, R-14). (§4)
- **"nothing is engineered"** — on an Upwork hold: no workaround, no second account, no trick. Send the passport and the bank statement within 24 h; log the days lost. The gate dates do not move. (F-16, §2)
- **Law of 100** — the first 100 proposals are about sending them, not about anyone liking them. (§6)
- **follow-up** — one message on day 5 to every unanswered proposal; write `f/u <date>` in the log's notes. (§6)
- **the paste-back block** — the `LONG-VOL READ` summary the skill prints at the end of every Sunday read. (the skill)

## The demand log
- **the demand log** — the table in the SCHEDULE: one row per Upwork post read or bid on, from Oct 9. It picks the sub-niche on Dec 7 and the product on Jan 6. (§6)
- **tag** — one of `agent-build · claude-code-setup · mcp-server · crm→ads sync · tracking · other`. (§6)
- **"repeats"** — ≥5 rows in 60 days asking for the same deliverable (the notes column, in the client's words). (§6)
- **the Dec 7 read** — the tag with the most `reply? = Y` becomes the profile title. (§2, F-04)
- **the Jan 6 read** — the top repeat becomes the product; else the default stands. (§6, R-14)
- **the tally** — one row per Sunday with Σ rows, bids, replies, hires, and rows per tag. (SCHEDULE)
- **Connects** — Upwork's bidding tokens, ≈14 per proposal, bought singly. $300 in total; no Freelancer Plus, no Boost, no ads. The cap fires F-07. (R-16, R-03)

## Money and Upwork words
- **DLB** — Direct to Local Bank: Upwork's withdrawal into the ACB VND account, $0.99 each, ~3 days to activate. Its "spread" is Upwork's USD→VND rate against the market rate; compare the first withdrawal with Vietcombank's rate. (§2, §5, U-05)
- **ACB** — the business hub bank. The VND payment account is open and PayOS settles into it. The USD account is not open; it waits for a USD inflow in P2. ([[Business Bank]])
- **PayOS** — the VND checkout rail of the EP platform, settling to ACB; webhook live since 2026-07-16. ([[Business Bank]], [[EP SaaS]])
- **Paddle** — the merchant-of-record billing rail for the product (software allowed, services banned); Polar is the fallback. (§3, U-07, U-08)
- **Regime A / Regime B** — A = registered business individual, ≈8% of gross. B = unregistered, taxed as wages, ≈21–27%. Plan on B until the route is proven. (§5, R-17)
- **W-8BEN** — the US tax form Upwork needs from a non-US freelancer. (§2)
- **the location check** — Upwork may ask for a recent bank statement in your name; keep one exported. (§2)
- **Form 01/BK-STK** — the 2026 notice to the tax authority of every account that receives business money. One of the two tax-adviser questions. ([[Business Bank]] §6)
- **the 10% PIT withholding** — payments ≥2M₫ to an individual without a labor contract carry a 10% personal-income-tax withholding duty; this is how the instructor's 50% must be declared. ([[Business Bank]] §6)
- **the tax adviser** — booked before the first payout, with U-01, Form 01/BK-STK and the instructor split on the list. (R-17, U-01)
